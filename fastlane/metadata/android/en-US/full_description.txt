Strategy & Roguelike brings 12 strategy and roguelike games into one app. Descend a misty dungeon by lantern light, finish a card expedition, hold out on a castaway shore until rescue; lead an army across hexes, play your squad in speed order in a courtyard, merge units in the hourglass arena and build a valley with an eye on every neighbor. Every level is generated and checked to be winnable by an AI player. Wins earn mastery points that unlock five lasting perks in each game.

• Runs: Lantern Depths, Deck Expedition, Castaway Shore, Dice Quest
• Battle and tactics: Hex Frontier, Courtyard Tactics, Squad Arena, Auto Warriors
• Build and plan: Valley Builder, Four Seasons Kingdom, Map Journal, Dungeon Master

Highlights
• 100,000 levels per game; every level is generated and checked to be winnable by an AI player.
• Ten difficulty steps: relaxed early levels, expert challenges later.
• Wins earn mastery points that unlock five lasting perks per game. Hints show the move an AI player would make.
• Daily and weekly levels, missions, achievements and 33 languages.
• Plays offline on phones, tablets and the web.
• No gambling, betting or 18+ content; suitable for all ages.

Purchases (such as removing ads) apply to every game in the app.
